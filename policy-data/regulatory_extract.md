> **Notice**: Illustrative sample, not legal text.

## 1. Automated Processing Restrictions
Under international privacy and cybersecurity governance frameworks, autonomous decision-making agents executing financial disbursements or data processing must adhere to explicit organizational authorization controls, strict auditability, and tamper-evident record keeping.

## 2. Personal Data Protection
Organizations must prevent accidental or unauthorized destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure of personal data. Systems must enforce technical blocks against unauthorized deletion of PII.
